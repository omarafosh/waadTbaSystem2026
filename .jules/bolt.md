## 2025-05-20 - O(N) In-Memory Filtering in RBAC Services
**Learning:** Repositories often expose standard methods that are misused to fetch all entries into memory (`findAll().stream()`) before filtering or mapping. For many-to-many relationships without bidirectional mapping (e.g., `User` has `roles`, but `Role` has no `users` collection), standard Spring Data `JpaRepository` methods are insufficient. To avoid loading the entire `User` table, custom JPQL queries must be defined on the driving entity (e.g., `SELECT u FROM User u JOIN u.roles r WHERE r.id = :roleId`).
**Action:** When asked to optimize an application, perform a codebase search for `findAll().stream()`. Replace any instance doing aggregations (`count`) or mapping/filtering (`filter`) with a targeted JPQL query. Always use `existsById` to maintain `ResourceNotFoundException` consistency instead of manually finding the entity just to throw an error.
