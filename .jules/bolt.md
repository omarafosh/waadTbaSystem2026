## 2024-05-21 - RoleManagementService In-Memory Filtering Bottleneck
**Learning:** In the RBAC module, relying on `userRepository.findAll().stream().filter(...)` to count users or get usernames for a specific role is extremely inefficient as it loads the entire `users` table into memory. The unidirectional mapping from `User` to `Role` means these queries must be driven from the `User` entity.
**Action:** Replaced stream filtering with targeted JPQL queries in `UserRepository` (`countByRolesId`, `findUsernamesByRolesId`). Always push counting and projection down to the database using JPQL when accessing unidirectional collections from the inverse side. Also, prefer `repository.existsById()` over `repository.findById().isPresent()` when only existence needs to be validated.
