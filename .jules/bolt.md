## 2026-06-08 - Fixing mock tests during optimization
**Learning:** Fixing one test failure in isolated mock test files (`UnifiedMemberServiceTest`) might unearth further peripheral mock errors (like `authorizationService` or `memberWorkflowHistoryRepository` being `null`). When a test fails with missing stubs or null pointers deep in the service logic that is not relevant to the test's immediate objective, using `@org.mockito.junit.jupiter.MockitoSettings(strictness = org.mockito.quality.Strictness.LENIENT)` combined with providing the missing `@Mock` dependencies safely resolves the cascading failures and restores a green build.
**Action:** When isolating tests in a large service, declare all required autowired dependencies as `@Mock` and apply the lenient setting at the class level if they are not the focus of the test, rather than trying to fix deep stubbing issues individually.
