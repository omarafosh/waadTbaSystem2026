## 2026-05-03 - [RoleManagementService Query Optimization]
**Learning:** Full-table in-memory filtering operations using `findAll().stream().map(...)` or `filter(...)` inside services are significant performance bottlenecks, especially when checking counts or mapping sub-fields. In the RBAC module, since the JPA relationship between `User` and `Role` is unidirectional from `User` (meaning `Role` doesn't have a mapped users collection), JPQL queries must drive from the `User` entity to function correctly (e.g., `SELECT u.username FROM User u JOIN u.roles r WHERE r.id = :roleId`).
**Action:** Replace `findAll().stream()` operations with targeted JPQL queries that push projections and aggregate counting directly to the database, ensuring queries align with JPA relationship directionality constraints.
